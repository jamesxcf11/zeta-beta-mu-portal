About Us

The founders, history, and fifty-five-year legacy of the Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity.

Zeta Beta Mu is a brotherhood of medical students and physicians founded on August 21, 1971 at the University of Santo Tomas Faculty of Medicine and Surgery. Meet the nineteen founders who started it all, then read how their vision grew into a fifty-five-year legacy of brotherhood, excellence, and service.

Our History

Five decades of Zeal for Brotherhood among Medics (1971–Present).

On August 21, 1971, a group of nineteen second-year medical students of the University of Santo Tomas Faculty of Medicine and Surgery came together with a shared vision: to establish a fraternity founded on brotherhood, excellence, and service. Most of the founding members belonged to Section B of their class, and their close friendship and shared aspirations became the foundation upon which Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity was built.

The idea of forming a fraternity had already been considered by some of the founders during their freshman year. According to Patrix Doctor Eduardo Geronimo, the opportunity to turn that idea into reality came during an unusual lockdown at the College of Medicine in 1971. Forming a pioneering group was not easy. Some classmates readily embraced the idea, while others were hesitant or indifferent. Nevertheless, the founders remained committed to establishing an organization of their own. On August 21, 1971, Zeta Beta Mu was formally given its name, representing “Zeal for Brotherhood among Medics.”

Growing a Brotherhood

The fraternity began with a small group of young medical students, but its membership soon expanded. Among the early recruits were Rod Guanlao, Lupo Guiyab, Rene Gonzales, and Gil Abelita, followed by succeeding junior members. What began as a group of classmates gradually developed into a lasting brotherhood that extended beyond the walls of the medical school.

From its earliest years, Zeta Beta Mu placed great importance on the bonds between its members. The term “Utol,” meaning brother, came to embody this relationship. For the founders, brotherhood was not something established overnight, but something earned and strengthened through years of camaraderie, respect, commitment, and shared experiences. As succeeding generations joined the fraternity, this sense of brotherhood became one of the defining traditions of Zeta Beta Mu.

The fraternity was guided by five Cardinal Pillars: Scholarship, Service, Sacrifice, Sociability, and Idealism. These pillars shaped the development of its members both within and beyond their medical education. Alongside brotherhood, the fraternity emphasized leadership, teamwork, personal development, and a commitment to helping others. Its activities gradually expanded beyond the development of its members to include initiatives that served communities in need.

Service Beyond the Brotherhood

Service became an enduring part of the fraternity’s identity. One of the early connections made by the fraternity was with World Vision Philippines, through which Zeta Beta Mu became involved in community-oriented activities. In the years that followed, the fraternity carried out medical missions and other charitable initiatives, extending the spirit of brotherhood beyond its membership and into the communities it served.

The fraternity’s growth continued through successive generations of Zetans. Its founders witnessed the organization evolve from a small group of medical students into a brotherhood whose members would go on to practice medicine throughout the Philippines and in different parts of the world. Despite the distance and changing circumstances that came with time, the connection between brothers remained an important part of the organization’s identity. As one of the founders recalled, a Zetan remains a Zetan wherever life may take him, with fellow brothers ready to extend support and fellowship.

The passage of time also strengthened the significance of the fraternity’s traditions. Generations of members carried forward the values established by the founders while contributing their own efforts to the continued development of the organization. Its progress was not the work of any single generation, but the result of the commitment, dedication, and collective efforts of the brothers who came before and those who continue to carry the fraternity forward.

Fifty Years and Beyond

On August 21, 2021, Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity marked its 50th anniversary. Five decades after its founding, the fraternity stood as a testament to the vision of its nineteen pioneers and to the generations of Zetans who sustained it. What began in 1971 as a small group of second-year medical students had grown into a continuing brotherhood built upon tradition, camaraderie, service, and a shared pursuit of excellence.

Today, the history of Zeta Beta Mu is carried not only in its founding date, but in the generations of brothers who have contributed to its growth. From its beginnings in the College of Medicine to its continuing presence through succeeding generations of Zetans, the fraternity remains rooted in the same spirit that brought its founders together in 1971: a lasting zeal for brotherhood among medics.
