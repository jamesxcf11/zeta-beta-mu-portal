Sociability

Sociability is an important part of Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity, reflecting the friendships, fellowship, and sense of community shared among our brothers. Through our social events and gatherings, we create opportunities to celebrate, connect with others, and strengthen the relationships that have always been an important part of the fraternity.

Each year, Zeta Beta Mu hosts two major social events, our Back-To-School Party and Year-Ender Party. These gatherings bring together students and young people for an evening of celebration, entertainment, and fellowship, creating an environment where new connections can be formed and lasting memories can be made.

Beyond our major events, our brothers also make time for outings, hangouts, and informal gatherings throughout the year. These moments allow our members to spend time together outside of our regular activities and strengthen the friendships and camaraderie within the brotherhood.

Through our events and everyday fellowship, Zeta Beta Mu continues to celebrate the spirit of sociability and the relationships that connect our brothers with the wider community.
