Service

Service is one of the enduring pillars of Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity. Through the years, our brothers have dedicated themselves to extending care and support beyond the fraternity, reaching communities across Metro Manila and various provinces through regular medical missions and outreach initiatives.

Among these efforts is our continuing partnership with Villa Maria Integrated School in Porac, Pampanga, a rural school serving a predominantly Aeta community. Through this partnership, Zeta Beta Mu supports the students and the community through educational assistance, gift-giving initiatives, and medical outreach, helping provide resources and opportunities where they are needed most. Villa Maria Integrated School has also been recognized for its work in bringing education to Aeta learners in the upland communities of Porac.

Alongside our work in Porac, our brothers continue to conduct regular medical missions in Metro Manila and various provinces, bringing healthcare and assistance directly to communities in need.

From the communities we serve in Porac to the many others reached through our medical missions, Zeta Beta Mu remains committed to putting brotherhood into action through service.
