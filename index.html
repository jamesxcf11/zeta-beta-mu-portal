About Us

Founded in 1971 at the University of Santo Tomas, the Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity is a brotherhood of medical students and physicians built upon generations of shared experiences, enduring traditions, and a lasting commitment to one another.

Guided by its five pillars, Scholarship, Service, Sociability, Sacrifice, and Idealism, ZBM seeks to foster personal growth, academic excellence, meaningful service, and genuine fellowship among its brothers. Through the years, its traditions have become an integral part of fraternity life, passed from one generation to the next and serving as a link between the brothers who came before and those who carry the fraternity forward.

More than five decades since its founding, Zeta Beta Mu continues to uphold its traditions while building new ones, cultivating a brotherhood that extends beyond medical school and remains with its members throughout their lives.

Ongoing Partnership

Supporting Villa Maria Integrated School

In the spirit of our pillar of Service, Zeta Beta Mu proudly supports Villa Maria Integrated School in Porac, Pampanga — a school dedicated to Aeta students. Through gift-giving drives, educational supplies, and medical outreach, our brothers help nurture the next generation of this community.
