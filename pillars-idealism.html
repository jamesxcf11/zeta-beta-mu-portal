Idealism

Idealism is one of the five pillars that has shaped Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity — the ethical and moral principles our brothers strive to uphold as physicians, leaders, and role models, carried forward across every generation of the fraternity.

Patrix Doctor John Hubert C. Pua

UST Faculty of Medicine and Surgery, Class of 2005 · Magna Cum Laude

“Message to follow”

Patrix Doctor Roberto Armando M. Cruz Jr.

UST Faculty of Medicine and Surgery, Class of 2022 · Cum Laude

“I’ve always considered myself an idealist, the kind who believes things can and should be better than they are. For most of my life, that belief felt fragile, until Zeta gave it a backbone, pairing conviction with discipline and the courage to act. With my brods beside me, idealism stopped being a dream I held and became a standard I live by.”

Patrix Doctor Renz Dwine L. Mendoza

UST Faculty of Medicine and Surgery, Class of 2023 · Cum Laude

“Idealism means incorporating the Zetan teachings into your daily life, so that even on an ordinary day, people can see the Zeta in you. As a physician, I hope to embody and uphold these ideals in my every day, as I handle patients, as I continue to learn to be the best physician I can be, and as I consistently guide my junior brods. Hail To Thee!”

Zeta Beta Mu Fraternity

A brotherhood forged through medicine, strengthened by tradition, and united by a commitment to one another and to the communities we serve.
